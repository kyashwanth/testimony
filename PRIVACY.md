# Testimony privacy policy

Testimony does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data, and makes no network requests.

**What it stores, on your device only**
- Screenshots of tabs you record or capture, step titles, your notes and annotations, page URLs and titles,
  and the ticket, environment and tester details you type in.
- Your two capture options and the list of sites you have allowed.

**What it does not do**
- It never reads the text you type into fields or the values of fields. Step titles name the field only
  ("Typed in the 'Email' field"). Screenshots show what is on screen, so they can contain typed text; use the
  redact tool before exporting if that matters.
- It only observes clicks and typing on sites you explicitly allowed, and only while recording.

**Control and deletion**
Data stays in your browser's local storage. "New session" moves the current session to History, which keeps
the last 5; deleting a history entry or removing the extension deletes the data. You can revoke any site's
access from the popup at any time.
